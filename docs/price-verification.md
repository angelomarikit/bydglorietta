# Price verification

Source: **BYD Price List, SRP as of 12 November 2026**, three pages. The PDF has no text layer. Figures below were read from the page images. Every price was confirmed on an isolated zoom of that price. Specification numbers for Sealion 6 DM-i, Shark 6 DMO, and every model on page 3 were confirmed again on isolated blocks. The other specification cells were read from the specification column.

Currency is Philippine pesos. The sheet says prices can change without notice, can vary by dealer and location, and provincial prices may be slightly higher because of transportation and logistics.

The price-list artwork has blank fields for dealership, sales consultant, and contact details. Those are meant to be filled per agent. They are not prices.

## Resolve before anything is published

1. **Two Seal 5 DM-i rows are both on this sheet.** Page 1 is **The New Seal 5 DM-i** (Essential ₱948,000, Dynamic ₱1,048,000, 5th Gen Super DM-i, 1,600 km combined, 7.42 kWh). Page 3 is **Seal 5 DM-i** (Dynamic ₱958,000, Premium ₱1,198,000, Super DM-i, 1,175 km / 1,240 km combined, 8.3 kWh / 18.3 kWh). They are stored as separate models. Do not merge them.
2. **Sealion 6 DM-i Premium is ₱1,798,000.** A first pass of the full page suggested ₱1,799,000. The isolated price reads ₱1,798,000. Dynamic at ₱1,548,000 matches the approved key visual, which says the Dynamic “price starts at ₱1,548,000”.
3. **The 2024–2025 Sealion 6 DM-i Refresh spec sheet is the previous model.** It says a combined range of about 1,000 km. The November 2026 price list and the approved key visual both say **1,200 km** combined for the current Sealion 6 DM-i. The key visual is headlined “vs. previous Sealion 6 DM-i model”. Use 1,200 km. Keep the refresh sheet in `reference-materials` only.
4. **eMAX 9 DM-i range is labeled NEDC, not combined.** Advanced is 95 km and Premium is 170 km. Every other DM-i row that means total hybrid range says “Combined Range”. Do not publish 95 km or 170 km as the car’s total range until ACMobility confirms what the label means.
5. **217 Ps and 300 Nm, and a 100 km full-EV range, are on the Sealion 6 key visual, not on the price list.** The previous refresh sheet also prints 217 Ps, so the power figure is consistent across the two Sealion 6 campaigns, and the range figure is not. Treat power, torque, full-EV range, V2L, and DiPilot as campaign claims (`marketingClaims` in the catalog) until a current specification table arrives.
6. **There is no full specification sheet for the current lineup.** The only current Sealion 6 “spec” art is a campaign one-pager. The InDesign spec sheet in the library is the previous refresh.
