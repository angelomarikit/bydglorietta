# Frame index

Use this as the source list for a design board. The production site is React, and these paths are the files that board should place.

## Master frames

1. **Header.** `public/assets/logos/byd-logo-grey.png` on white, or the white logo on a photo. Dealer name “Glorietta” is set in type. Do not place `reference-materials/01-branding/byd-glorietta/byd-glorietta-lockup.png`.
2. **Distributor line.** `public/assets/logos/acmobility-logo-full-color.png`, smaller than the BYD mark.
3. **Sealion 6 product hero.** `public/assets/vehicles/sealion-6-dm-i/hero/sealion-6-dm-i-delan-black-exterior-01.jpg`. Full bleed, no crop of the car. 1080 pixels wide is the native size.
4. **Campaign banner.** `public/assets/banners/sealion-6-dm-i-main-kv.jpg`. Leave its own headline and price readable. Do not stack another headline on top of the type.
5. **Exterior row.** The five files in `public/assets/vehicles/sealion-6-dm-i/exterior/`.
6. **Spin.** `gallery/sealion-6-dm-i-delan-black-360-01.jpg` through `06`, in that order.
7. **Interior.** `interior-01` through `interior-05`, then premium `02` through `05`. There is no premium `01`.
8. **Lineup.** Sixteen models from `docs/vehicle-catalog.json`, price-list order. Only Sealion 6 has a photograph. Other cards are type, price, and an empty image state.
9. **Agent bar.** Name, portrait, phone, Messenger or WhatsApp. No agent photos were in the asset drop. Use a placeholder silhouette, not a vehicle image.
10. **Forms.** Inquiry, and test drive. Both belong to the agent whose URL is open.
